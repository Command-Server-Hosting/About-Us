# How It All Started

Command Server Hosting started with a simple problem. Cam and Vincent were playing BeamMP together, but they were tired of relying on other people's servers — no custom mods, no control, no freedom.

> “Why not help others do the same?”
> — The moment everything changed

So Cam took matters into his own hands and set up his own BeamMP server. What started as a simple fix became the spark for something much bigger.

Progress was slow at first, but it didn't take long before someone joined our Discord and became our very first customer. That first sale gave us the confidence to keep building — improving our platform, switching to Pterodactyl for better server management, and making countless upgrades.

Today, we help gamers worldwide build their servers, their way.
